How to Read This SOP

Every step follows: INPUT → DO → CHECK → OUTPUT → NEXT. A failed CHECK loops you back to fix it; a passed CHECK moves you forward. Do not start the next step until the current output is ready.

STEP 1 Scrape Job Postings

Goal: Find recent job postings matching Focused Outreach criteria. Tools: Apify (LinkedIn + Indeed actors), Job Title Keyword file, Google Drive.

(NEVER Volunteer / Internship)
Experience: Entry, Associate, Mid-senior
Work type: Remote, Hybrid

Government administration, Government relations.

Golden Rule

Never start a step by guessing what to do. For every step ask:

  1. What file/input should I have?
  2. What tool should I use?
  3. What filters/settings should I use?
  4. What should the output look like?
  5. Where does the output go next?

If any of these five things are unclear — stop and check the SOP before continuing.
